LOCKOUT/TAGOUT PROCEDURE - MANUFACTURING SAFETY PROTOCOL

Document ID: SP-001
Revision: 3.2
Date: 2024-01-15
Department: Safety & Compliance

OVERVIEW
========
This procedure establishes the minimum requirements for the lockout of energy isolating devices whenever maintenance or servicing is done on machines or equipment.

SCOPE
=====
This procedure applies to all manufacturing equipment including:
- CNC machines (Models: PL1-CNC-001, PL2-CNC-002, PL3-CNC-003)
- Assembly line conveyors
- Hydraulic presses
- Robotic systems
- Packaging equipment

ENERGY SOURCES
==============
The following energy sources must be controlled:
1. Electrical energy
2. Mechanical energy (springs, elevated machine members, rotating flywheels)
3. Hydraulic energy
4. Pneumatic energy
5. Chemical energy
6. Thermal energy

LOCKOUT PROCEDURE
=================
1. PREPARATION
   - Notify all affected employees
   - Identify all energy sources
   - Shut down equipment using normal stopping procedures

2. ISOLATION
   - Locate and operate all energy isolating devices
   - Dissipate or restrain stored energy
   - Verify isolation by testing controls

3. LOCKOUT/TAGOUT
   - Apply lockout devices to energy isolating devices
   - Attach tags identifying the employee and reason for lockout
   - Ensure each employee applies their own lock

4. VERIFICATION
   - Attempt to start equipment to verify effective isolation
   - Return controls to neutral position after verification

REMOVAL PROCEDURE
=================
1. Inspect work area to ensure tools are removed
2. Check that all employees are clear of equipment
3. Verify controls are in neutral position
4. Remove lockout/tagout devices (only by person who applied them)
5. Re-energize equipment

EMERGENCY CONTACT
=================
Safety Manager: Ext. 2911
Maintenance Supervisor: Ext. 2922
Emergency Services: 911

TRAINING REQUIREMENTS
====================
All employees must complete LOTO training before working on equipment.
Annual refresher training is mandatory.

⚠️ CRITICAL SAFETY WARNING ⚠️
Failure to follow this procedure may result in serious injury or death.
Never remove another person's lock or tag.
