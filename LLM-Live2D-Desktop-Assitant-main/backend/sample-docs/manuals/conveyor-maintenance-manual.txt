SYSTEM OVERVIEW
===============
The assembly line conveyor system consists of:
- Main drive motor (5 HP, 1800 RPM)
- Reduction gearbox (20:1 ratio)
- Drive pulley (12" diameter)
- Tail pulley (8" diameter)
- Belt tensioning system
- Safety guards and emergency stops

SPECIFICATIONS
==============
Belt Type: Polyurethane, 24" wide
Belt Speed: 15 feet per minute (variable)
Load Capacity: 500 lbs distributed
Operating Temperature: 32°F to 120°F
Power Requirements: 480V, 3-phase, 60Hz

DAILY MAINTENANCE CHECKLIST
============================
□ Visual inspection of belt condition
□ Check belt tracking and alignment
□ Verify proper belt tension
□ Inspect safety guards and emergency stops
□ Check for unusual noise or vibration
□ Clean debris from belt surface
□ Verify proper lubrication of bearings

WEEKLY MAINTENANCE
==================
1. BELT INSPECTION
   - Check for cuts, tears, or excessive wear
   - Measure belt thickness at multiple points
   - Inspect belt edges for fraying
   - Document wear patterns

2. DRIVE SYSTEM CHECK
   - Inspect motor coupling alignment
   - Check gearbox oil level
   - Verify drive pulley condition
   - Test variable speed control

3. SAFETY SYSTEMS
   - Test all emergency stop buttons
   - Verify safety guard integrity
   - Check warning lights and alarms
   - Test pull-cord emergency stops

MONTHLY MAINTENANCE
===================
1. LUBRICATION SCHEDULE
   Drive Motor Bearings: High-temp grease (Part #: LUB-HT-001)
   Gearbox: SAE 90 gear oil (Part #: OIL-SAE90-5GAL)
   Tail Pulley Bearings: Multi-purpose grease (Part #: LUB-MP-002)

2. BELT TENSIONING
   Proper tension: 50-60 lbs deflection force
   Adjustment procedure:
   a. Stop conveyor and apply LOTO
   b. Loosen tail pulley mounting bolts
   c. Adjust tension using threaded rods
   d. Check belt tracking after adjustment
   e. Tighten mounting bolts to 85 ft-lbs

3. ELECTRICAL INSPECTION
   - Check motor connections
   - Inspect control panel components
   - Test variable frequency drive (VFD)
   - Verify proper grounding

TROUBLESHOOTING GUIDE
=====================

PROBLEM: Belt slipping
CAUSES: Low tension, worn pulley, contaminated belt
SOLUTION: Adjust tension, clean pulleys, replace if worn

PROBLEM: Belt tracking off-center
CAUSES: Misaligned pulleys, uneven loading, worn components
SOLUTION: Realign pulleys, check loading, inspect for wear

PROBLEM: Excessive noise
CAUSES: Worn bearings, misalignment, loose components
SOLUTION: Lubricate bearings, check alignment, tighten fasteners

PROBLEM: Motor overheating
CAUSES: Overloading, poor ventilation, electrical issues
SOLUTION: Reduce load, clean motor, check electrical connections

SAFETY WARNINGS
===============
⚠️ DANGER: Moving machinery can cause serious injury or death
- Always apply lockout/tagout before maintenance
- Never reach over or under moving conveyor
- Keep loose clothing and jewelry away from moving parts
- Use proper lifting techniques for heavy components

⚠️ ELECTRICAL HAZARD: 480V can be fatal
- Only qualified electricians should work on electrical systems
- Verify power is off before working on electrical components
- Use proper PPE when working with electrical systems
